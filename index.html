Signed in:

Collect together

    Each person signs in with their own email. Invited members can add, edit and remove finds and photographs in this shared collection. They don't get access to your Supabase dashboard or GitHub repository.

    Import a UK species catalogue

    Import an NBN Atlas species CSV or a compatible CSV with scientificName, commonName and optional family, kingdom, rank columns. Names are added to the built-in starter collection; duplicate species are merged.

    To publish your full catalogue to every device, replace data/species.json in GitHub with the exported JSON.

    Your collection backup

    Download a backup containing your finds, notes and compressed photos. Keep it somewhere safe. You can restore it on another iPhone, iPad or computer.

    Important to know

    When you're signed in, MycoDex synchronises your local discoveries to Supabase. Shared collections allow separate email accounts to contribute to the same finds and photographs. Offline changes sync when connectivity returns. Until the first successful cloud sync, keep a local backup of your discoveries.
